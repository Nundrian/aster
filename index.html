Before changing anything

Work out what the job actually is.

The goal, the material, the boundaries and the things that must survive all matter. ASTER uses them to decide what belongs in the job before deciding what belongs in the change.

THE
JOB
What are we trying to achieve? What must stay fixed? What material exists? What has to come out? What tools are available? What is outside the job?

TRIAGE

Look beyond the words

Not every prompt problem is actually a prompt problem.

The wording may be fine. The real friction could sit in the structure, context, tools, model, hand-off between stages or the way the result is being checked.

Purposeful change

The change needs a reason.

Finding a problem does not automatically mean adding another instruction. Sometimes the useful answer is to clarify, simplify, restructure, research — or leave it alone.

  • More instructions ≠ better
  • Another agent ≠ better
  • Another reasoning round ≠ better
  • Shorter ≠ better by default

What can you actually do with it?

Improve. Investigate. Simplify. Create. Merge.

Use ASTER on something that already works, something that failed, something that grew too complicated — or a workflow that doesn’t exist yet.

Improve

Preserve the useful parts and strengthen the weak ones.

Investigate

Trace a failed result back through the workflow instead of guessing.

Simplify

Remove complexity that no longer earns its place.

Create

Build around the job, constraints and available material.

Merge

Combine compatible parts from different workflows into one coherent hybrid.
