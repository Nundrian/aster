Before adding something else

Don’t fix it. Yet…

First find out what to fix first.

Workflows have a habit of growing:

  1. another instruction;
  2. another stage;
  3. another workaround;
  4. another instruction to compensate for the workaround.
The message isn’t “your workflow is bad.”It’s: find out what actually needs changing.

Five useful jobs

Give your AI a better way to work.

ASTER can work with something established, something that failed, something that grew too large or something that doesn’t exist yet.

  1. Improve

    Improve something that already works. Preserve the useful parts and strengthen the weak ones.

  2. Investigate

    Use the workflow, its input and the failed result to work back towards the likely cause instead of rewriting whatever looks suspicious.

  3. Simplify

    Look at what is genuinely needed and remove complexity that is no longer helping.

  4. Create

    Start with the job, what matters and what must remain fixed. Build the workflow around what the AI actually needs to accomplish.

  5. Merge

    Take useful, compatible parts from different workflows and create a coherent hybrid—even when those workflows perform different jobs.

How ASTER works

Enough structure to guide the work, without turning every job into a technical exercise.

Using ASTER is simple

Upload it. Point it at the job.

  1. Upload the ASTER ZIPInclude the prompt, workflow or material you want it to work with.
  2. Tell the AI what to readAsk it to read 00_READ_FIRST.md and follow the instructions.
  3. Say what you want doneImprove, investigate, simplify, create or merge.
Improve a workflow 
Read 00_READ_FIRST.md in the ZIP and follow its instructions. I also want you to improve this workflow. It already works well, but I want it to be more reliable and easier to use.
Merge two different workflows 
Read 00_READ_FIRST.md in the ZIP and follow its instructions. I also want you to take the best parts of these two workflows, merge them into one and optimise the result.
Investigate a failed result 
Read 00_READ_FIRST.md in the ZIP and follow its instructions. I also want you to find out why this workflow produced the attached result and fix the cause.

Especially useful

A failed result.

A useful failure gives ASTER something real to trace. It can compare the workflow, the input and what came out instead of guessing where the problem might be.
