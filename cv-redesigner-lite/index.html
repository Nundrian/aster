What it does

  • Uses your completed CV as the source document.
  • Tells the AI to preserve the wording rather than rewrite, shorten or tidy it up.
  • Gives you four current Microsoft Word template choices.
  • Gives you three fixed professional colour schemes.
  • Lets you choose PDF, Word, or both.
  • Requires a final check for missing, moved, duplicated or altered content.
  • Supports ATS-minded single- and two-column options.

What it doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t invent achievements.
  • It doesn’t shorten your employment history to make the page look neater.
  • It doesn’t quietly rewrite your tone or wording.
  • It doesn’t turn the job into a bespoke graphic-design exercise.
  • You don’t need to learn PDF editing or document design before you start.
